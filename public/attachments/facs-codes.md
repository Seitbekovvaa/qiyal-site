# FACS Codes — Facial Action Coding System

FACS (Facial Action Coding System) by Paul Ekman & Wallace Friesen is the standard anatomical coding system for facial movements. Every visible change on the face is decomposed into **Action Units (AUs)**.

Intensity is scored from **A** (trace) to **E** (maximum).

## Main Action Units (Facial)

| AU  | Name                      | Primary Muscle(s)                              | Appearance |
|-----|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------|----------|
| 0   | Neutral face               | —                                              | Resting face |
| 1   | Inner Brow Raiser         | Frontalis (pars medialis)                      | Inner brows raised |
| 2   | Outer Brow Raiser         | Frontalis (pars lateralis)                     | Outer brows raised |
| 4   | Brow Lowerer              | Corrugator supercilii, Depressor supercilii, Depressor glabellae | Brows lowered & drawn together |
| 5   | Upper Lid Raiser          | Levator palpebrae superioris                   | Upper eyelid raised, more sclera visible |
| 6   | Cheek Raiser              | Orbicularis oculi (pars orbitalis)             | Cheeks raised, crow’s feet |
| 7   | Lid Tightener             | Orbicularis oculi (pars palpebralis)           | Eyelids tightened, narrowed aperture |
| 9   | Nose Wrinkler             | Levator labii superioris alaeque nasi          | Nose wrinkled, upper lip raised |
| 10  | Upper Lip Raiser          | Levator labii superioris                       | Upper lip raised |
| 11  | Nasolabial Deepener       | Zygomaticus minor                              | Deepens nasolabial furrow |
| 12  | Lip Corner Puller         | Zygomaticus major                              | Lip corners pulled up and laterally (smile) |
| 13  | Sharp Lip Puller          | Levator anguli oris (Caninus)                  | Sharp upward pull of lip corner |
| 14  | Dimpler                   | Buccinator                                     | Dimples appear |
| 15  | Lip Corner Depressor      | Depressor anguli oris (Triangularis)           | Lip corners pulled down |
| 16  | Lower Lip Depressor       | Depressor labii inferioris                     | Lower lip pulled down |
| 17  | Chin Raiser               | Mentalis                                       | Chin boss raised, lower lip pushed up |
| 18  | Lip Pucker                | Incisivii labii superioris & inferioris        | Lips puckered |
| 20  | Lip Stretcher             | Risorius + Platysma                            | Lips stretched laterally |
| 22  | Lip Funneler              | Orbicularis oris                               | Lips funneled forward |
| 23  | Lip Tightener             | Orbicularis oris                               | Lips tightened and narrowed |
| 24  | Lip Pressor               | Orbicularis oris                               | Lips pressed together |
| 25  | Lips Part                 | — (relaxation or depressor labii)              | Lips parted |
| 26  | Jaw Drop                  | Masseter relaxed + others                      | Jaw dropped (teeth separated) |
| 27  | Mouth Stretch             | Pterygoids, Digastric                          | Mouth stretched wide open |
| 28  | Lip Suck                  | Orbicularis oris                               | Lips sucked inward |

## Notes for Practical Use

- **Unilateral** actions are marked with L (left) or R (right), or U when side is unspecified.
- Combinations create emotions (see previous facial-anatomy and microexpressions references).
- Intensity scoring (A–E) is critical for subtle vs strong expressions and for microexpressions.
- In character design and animation: identify the active AUs first, then decide intensity and stylization level while preserving the character’s unique facial structure.
