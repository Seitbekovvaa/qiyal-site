# Примеры cinematic-промптов для Grok

Готовые рабочие шаблоны с учётом физики, камеры, взгляда и типичных проблем Grok.

## 1. Медленный эмоциональный портрет

```
Close-up of a 30-year-old man with tired eyes and short dark hair, sitting in a dimly lit room. He slowly turns his head slightly to the left, looking off-screen with a heavy, contemplative expression. Soft natural window light from the side, subtle dust in the air. Camera: very slow subtle push-in, eye-level, 50mm lens feel. Cinematic, muted colors, shallow depth of field. Serious neutral face, not looking at camera, no smile.
```

## 2. Динамичный бег (физика)

```
A young woman in a dark hoodie sprinting down a rain-soaked city street at night. Strong forward lean, powerful stride, real weight and momentum in every step, feet planting firmly on the wet asphalt with visible impact. Hood and hair move with secondary motion. Camera: low side tracking shot following her at the same speed, slight handheld energy. Neon reflections on the ground, cinematic contrast. Looking straight ahead, focused expression, not looking at camera, no smile.
```

## 3. Драка / удар

```
Medium shot of two men fighting in a narrow alley. The man on the left throws a heavy right hook. Clear weight transfer from the back foot, hip rotation, and full-body commitment into the punch. Impact is solid. Camera: locked medium shot with a slight push-in on the moment of contact. Gritty cinematic lighting, high contrast. Both characters focused on each other, not looking at camera.
```

## 4. Тихий диалоговый момент

```
Medium two-shot of a woman and a man sitting across a wooden table in a quiet café. The woman leans forward slightly and speaks, looking directly at the man. The man listens, eyes fixed on her face, subtle reaction in his expression. Soft natural daylight from the side window. Camera: static, eye-level, gentle shallow depth of field. Natural, restrained performances. Neither character looks at the camera.
```

## 5. Multi-shot (3 шота)

```
Shot 1 (0-3s): Wide shot of a man standing alone on a foggy bridge at dawn. He is still, looking into the distance. Locked-off camera, cold blue light, cinematic atmosphere.

Hard cut.

Shot 2 (3-6s): Medium close-up of the same man. He slowly lowers his gaze. Soft side light. Very slow subtle push-in. Serious expression, looking down, not at camera.

Hard cut.

Shot 3 (6-9s): Close-up of his hand resting on the metal railing, fingers slightly tightening. Shallow depth of field, cool tones.
```

## 6. Мотивированное движение камеры

```
A detective walks slowly through a dark abandoned office at night. Camera starts behind him in a medium wide shot and smoothly tracks forward with him as he moves deeper into the room, ending in a medium shot. Motivated tracking movement, eye-level, 35mm. Practical desk lamps create pools of warm light in the darkness. Tense, quiet atmosphere. Character looking ahead into the room, never at the camera.
```

## 7. Быстрый экшен + физика

```
A man in a tactical jacket runs and leaps across a gap between two rooftops. Clear run-up, powerful push-off from the left leg, body fully committed forward, realistic arc of the jump, landing with bent knees absorbing the impact. Camera: dynamic side tracking shot that follows the jump, slight low angle. Overcast daylight, cinematic motion. Focused determined expression, looking at the opposite rooftop, not at camera.
```

## Ключевые правила, которые используются во всех примерах

- Точное направление взгляда
- Явное выражение лица («no smile», «serious», «focused»)
- Физика и вес
- Одно ясное движение камеры
- Освещение
- Ограничение «not looking at camera»
